What can I bring to the table? What value could I add?

Vision

This may be one of my strongest attributes. As my knowledge grows, as I reflect, insights start to emerge that connect the world together, and as I mentioned before, they take the forms of systems. Being able to see systems leads to vision: what is and what should (or could) be. The is-ought gap, after all, isn’t as elusive as we once believed.

This vision seeks to align ethical frameworks with economic realities. It seeks to unify both divided forces, to eliminate the friction between opposing moral frameworks.

Strategy

Strategy turns vision into reality. An idea without action remains in the ether. Being able to strategize means giving something conceptual a tangible form. This requires knowing when, how and where to implement something, and who is competent enough to fulfill the tasks. It also means developing feedback loops, so the benefits are always compounding in every direction while the drawbacks are constantly being reduced. It means developing systems whose inputs feed the outputs, and outputs feed the inputs.

Alignment

I recognize the importance of aligning the various departments and roles within the healthcare field. These aren’t exclusive domains, they are interdependent. Alignment of their goals through their duties and responsibilities (along with appropriate incentives) is crucial for efficiency.

Impact Potential

I know hospitals are typically not traditional businesses. As I understand, hospitals operate with thin margins, from 1-5% on average in the Duluth area. Because my method results in a surplus-generating engine, it is estimated to increase operating margins three- to four-fold, annually. And that is just the operating margin.

Potential and targeted benefits of a successful pilot:

  • Expand Care Access
  • Eliminate Claim Denials
  • Improve Employer–Employee Relations
  • Improve Quality of Care
  • Create Local Employment
  • Relieve Infrastructure Dependency
  • Reduce Per-Person Cost
  • Improve Financial Security

This framework is structured to preserve executive benefits while distributing bonuses across the hospital ecosystem. Detailed financials and projections are available for confidential pilot discussions.
