Hobbies

Systems Architecture

I spend my free time diagramming system interactions, mapping and innovating complex concepts or systems. Because I tend to think in terms of words and process interactions, I naturally organize lists of terms to diagram interrelationships. This became a hobby after becoming enamored by the insights I could derive from the data during meditation. I have always felt that the resulting vision is the path to ameliorating various problems our society faces.

Woodworking

Woodworking ultimately culminates in a rudimentary understanding of systems. From gathering raw materials, to preparing the wood, working the wood, applying finish to the wood—the success of these processes are all intertwined and dependent on the quality of care given to each step. Learning to work with wood in a production capacity taught me how to express my aesthetic creativity in a form that is fully functional. It led me to unify the arts and the sciences into one cohesive whole, not two separate things in friction with each other.
