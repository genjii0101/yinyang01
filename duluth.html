Discover Duluth & UMD

What makes Duluth great?

Locale

Duluth seems balanced in terms of locale. Never too far from the serenity found in a forest and the soothing chirps of birds, yet close enough to urban areas when loneliness strikes. People seem to be friendlier in semi-rural areas, the pace of life seems slower. In an urban environment, you can stock up on supplies and have some hustle and bustle, then switch gears in a rural one to meditate and reflect on your day. I think the geography of Duluth also lends itself to interest. A large lake to take a dip your toes into while searching for agates on the shores, craggy hillsides, beautiful scenes of the city to admire. What's not to love?

Potential

I also see the city of Duluth and the surrounding area to be rife with potential. The rural areas are spacious, and the city itself is not a metropolis. Raw resources, labor, education institutions, and few competing businesses mean an environment in which inputs are available, but processes are not refined to create productive, lucrative outputs. This suits my endeavors perfectly, perhaps I can lead by example how to respect nature while also acknowledging human need.

Experiences in Proximity to Nature

I love feeding wild animals. If you have patience and a gentle spirit, Duluth is a great place to develop some unconventional friendships. I've had plenty of fun scrounging around the surrounding areas of Duluth for unique character materials to use for my woodworking hobby. Agate hunting, burl spotting, vine-squeezed twisty walking stick "poaching," fun!

Why UMD?

I chose the University of Minnesota, Duluth campus for a variety of reasons, but two are of greater significance to me.

First, the University has a reputation with my alumni parents who attended both the Duluth and Minneapolis campuses, one graduating from the Minneapolis campus and the other from the Duluth campus. A part of me wants to carry this tradition through to the next generation so each generation may gradually improve its excellence.

Second, the Duluth campus is large enough to provide unique experiences with diverse individuals while not succumbing to the impersonal indifference of a larger campus. There is value lost when interactions become impersonal from the effects of population size. When we interact and network with people on a professional AND personal level, we tend to dig a little more deeply into our potential to improve each other's well-being alongside the broader social value and utility we bring through the products of those dealings. The Duluth campus synergizes these two: empathy and conscientiousness (industriousness).

Study Spaces at UMD

The University of Minnesota Duluth offers plenty of quiet spaces to study. This is crucial for producing quality work throughout an education experience. By reducing distraction, a student can focus their attention on the task at hand, and UMD provides ample space designed and designated for this purpose. Not only do they provide comfortable options, they also offer private rooms for group projects without disturbing other students.
